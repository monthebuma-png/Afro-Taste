# Project Title

**AfroTaste – The Taste of African Dishes**

# Project Idea

**AfroTaste** will be a modern and responsive marketing website for a local African restaurant. The website will show traditional African dishes, drinks and the restaurant's services.

Visitors will be able to explore popular meals, view prices, learn more about the restaurant and contact the restaurant for orders or inquiries.

The website will include a **hero section, food menu, about section and contact section**. It will also be designed to work well on both computers and mobile devices.

The goal of AfroTaste is to **promote African dishes online, attract new customers, and give the restaurant a strong digital presence**.
